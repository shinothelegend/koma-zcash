# KOMA / Zcash

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated implementation of the existing KOMA web product. A small Node server and SQLite maintain private invoices and reader access; vanilla semantic HTML/CSS/JavaScript inherit KOMA's established comic-print identity. User approved building directly from that design.

## Users
Comic creators launching a series and readers who want to back, unlock and influence stories without a public payment-history profile.

## Product Purpose
A Zecathon Wildcard adaptation of KOMA, with a redesigned launchpad, Zcash payments, creator publishing and protected episode reading. User requested the launchpad as well as the reader.

## Positioning
Shielded ZEC travels directly to the creator. A read-only incoming viewing key checks a unique invoice memo and amount. Verified support confers app-managed reading and canon-voting rights.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Testnet only. No invented NFTs, coins, on-chain voting or trustless enforcement. No public list of readers, payment txids or invoice memos. Browser sessions and receipt secrets are bearer access. Creator administration is authenticated. The publisher and payment verifier can see payment context. The verification service is not a full validating node. AI demonstration spend is capped at US$0.50 by the user.

## Brand Commitments
Retain KOMA's existing comic-print style and assets. Original projects and existing uncommitted work must remain unchanged. Record prior-art reuse accurately.

## Evidence on Hand
Original KOMA source and real generated comic pages, plus Hanko's existing testnet verification service. No new KOMA Zcash payment or deployed service is claimed until checked. The supplied Drive folder is initially empty.

## Product Principles
- Reading content stays behind a verified entitlement.
- Missing setup fails closed with a useful explanation.
- Real payments and real provider output only.
- Explain the difference between shielded chain data and the app's trusted records.
