# KOMA inherited visual world

KOMA's existing visual authority is `/Volumes/Extreme SSD/Projects/koma-monad/src/app/globals.css`: black stock, warm paper, orange printing ink, yellow highlights, giant condensed headlines and comic panel rules. This adaptation retains that world rather than introducing a new identity.

The shelf is an Experience surface with comic covers leading; the launch studio, invoice and reader are Operate surfaces. The primary action is launch/back/unlock, with testnet status and privacy limits readable near payment. Mobile stacks the spread into a single page; keyboard focus, reduced motion and semantic controls are required. No invented activity or success metrics.

Code-first workflow was approved by the user. Review at desktop and 375px, fix material defects in one batch, then request a fresh finish review.
